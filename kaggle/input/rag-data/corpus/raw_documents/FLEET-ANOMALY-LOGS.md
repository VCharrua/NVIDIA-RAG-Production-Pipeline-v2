## Anomaly Log INC-2021-DDG-101-1 - DDG-101

Incident Report Record: INC-2021-DDG-101-1
Vessel: DDG-101 | Date: 2021-01-15 | Location: Machinery Compartment 4-180-0-E
Anomaly Category: Structural Hull Micro-fracture
Technical Narrative: During underway operations in Sea State 5, automated telemetry flagged an abnormal condition. Physical inspection confirmed Frame 142 web frame fillet weld cracking. System operating hours at time of incident: 620300. Total vessel downtime: 36 hours. Immediate remediation performed: Ultrasonic gouging and insert plate welding. Root cause analysis confirmed fatigue cycling and marine atmospheric exposure. Incident resolved and signed off by Chief Engineer.
