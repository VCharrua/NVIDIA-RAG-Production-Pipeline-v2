## Shipboard Waste Heat Recovery & Steam Generator HRSG (P.1)

Technical Manual Section 9.10.1 - Shipboard Waste Heat Recovery & Steam Generator HRSG (Part 1):
Detailed operating procedure and maintenance instructions for shipboard installations. Dual-pressure finned economizer tubes, soot blower pneumatic sequencing, feed-water deaerator dissolved oxygen < 7 ppb. Verify secondary containment integrity and ensure environmental drainage seals are certified to Level 4 marine resistance. Reference maintenance card MRC-109-001 for full technician signoff.
