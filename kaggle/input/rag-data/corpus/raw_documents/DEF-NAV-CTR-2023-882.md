# Naval Vessel Refit & Modernization Master Services Agreement (MSA-2023-882)
**Document ID:** DEF-NAV-CTR-2023-882 | **Type:** procurement_contract

## Termination for Default and Breach Notifications

Section 5.1 Termination for Default: The Procuring Authority may, by written notice of default to the Prime Contractor, terminate the whole or any operational portion of this Contract if the Contractor fails to deliver supplies or perform services within the time specified in Schedule C, or fails to make progress so as to endanger performance. The Procuring Authority shall issue a 30-day Cure Notice. If such failure is not cured within thirty (30) calendar days after receipt of notice, termination shall take effect immediately. Rights and remedies regarding default damages are governed specifically by Section 5.1.1 and quantified according to Appendix B.

## Liquidated Damages for Late Milestone Deliverables

Section 5.1.1 Liquidated Damages: In the event of unexcused delay in completing Milestone Deliverables designated in Schedule A, the Prime Contractor shall pay to the Procuring Authority liquidated damages rather than a penalty. The per-diem damage rate shall be assessed at $25,000 per calendar day of delay until final acceptance, subject to the overall monetary liability cap stipulated in Appendix B (Liquidated Damages Schedule), which explicitly caps cumulative liquidated damages at fifteen percent (15%) of the total allocable Task Order Value.

## Appendix B: Liquidated Damages and Liability Ceiling Table

Appendix B - Liquidated Damages Framework:

Special Provision B.4: If termination occurs pursuant to Section 5.1, liquidated damages cease accruing on the date of notice, and reprocurement costs shall be offset against any unpaid milestone balances retained under Section 9.3.

## Indemnification, Defense of Claims, and Exclusions

Section 8.4 Indemnification: The Contractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Procuring Authority and its naval personnel from and against any third-party claims, liabilities, or losses arising from bodily injury, tangible property damage, or infringement of proprietary patents. However, the Contractor's indemnification obligations under this Section are subject to the liability ceilings stipulated in Appendix E (Risk Allocation Framework) and shall not apply where claims arise solely from government-furnished equipment (GFE) as cataloged in Annex C.

## Appendix E: Risk Allocation and Aggregate Liability Ceilings

Appendix E - Contractual Liability Limits: 
Except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breaches of Section 12 (Confidentiality and National Security), the Contractor's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be strictly capped at 100% of the aggregate fees paid under the preceding 12 months, or $50,000,000, whichever amount is lesser. Neither party shall be liable for consequential, indirect, or loss-of-readiness damages.

## Latent Defect Remediation and Warranty Duration

Section 14.2 Latent Defects: The standard warranty period shall extend for thirty-six (36) months following the Sea Acceptance Trials Certificate. For latent defects in major propulsion machinery and hull structural welds not discoverable upon reasonable post-delivery inspection, the warranty window shall extend to seventy-two (72) months. The Contractor must dispatch an authorized engineering strike team to the home port within 48 hours of latent defect notification pursuant to Annex D protocols.

## Key Personnel Retention and Replacement Approval

Contractor may not substitute Key Personnel without 45 days prior notice to Contracting Officer.

## Security Clearance & Classified Enclave Access

All personnel entering engineering spaces must possess active SECRET clearance per NISPOM standards.

## Termination for Convenience of Government

Government may terminate at convenience upon 60 days notice. Contractor awarded reasonable wind-down costs per FAR 52.249-2.

## Quality Assurance & ISO-9001 Compliance

Contractor shall maintain ISO-9001:2015 certification and submit to unannounced quarterly DCMA surveillance audits.

## Retainage & Milestone Holdback Provisions

A retainage fee of 10% shall be withheld from all interim milestone disbursements until final Sea Trials signoff.

## Force Majeure and Supply Chain Excusable Delays

Excusable delays require formal notification within 10 days of event inception, supported by certified carrier manifests.

## Proprietary Data Rights and Commercial Off-The-Shelf Software

Unlimited rights granted in technical manuals; restricted computer software rights retained by Contractor per DFARS 252.227-7014.

## Environmental Compliance and Hazardous Waste Abatement

All lead paint and asbestos remediation must adhere strictly to EPA Title 40 CFR and maritime disposal permits.

## Dispute Resolution and Armed Services Board of Contract Appeals

Contractual disputes shall be adjudicated pursuant to the Contract Disputes Act of 1978 before the ASBCA.
