## Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) Shielding Enclosures (P.1)

Technical Manual Section 9.4.1 - Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) Shielding Enclosures (Part 1):
Detailed operating procedure and maintenance instructions for shipboard installations. Gasket conductivity standards, MIL-STD-461G compliance testing, cable braid ground bond resistance < 2.5 milliohms. Step 1: Inspect mechanical interfaces and confirm operational clearance within nominal tolerance of ±0.05 mm. Verify secondary containment integrity and ensure environmental drainage seals are certified to Level 4 marine resistance. Reference maintenance card MRC-103-001 for full technician signoff.
