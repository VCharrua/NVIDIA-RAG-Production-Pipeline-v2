# International Maritime Organization Statutory Directives (MARPOL Annex VI & SOLAS Ch II)
**Document ID:** REG-IMO-2024 | **Type:** regulatory_framework

---

## MARPOL Annex VI Regulation 13 - NOx Technical Code Tier III

Regulation 13.5: In Emission Control Areas (ECAs), marine diesel engines with power output > 130 kW installed on ships constructed on or after 1 January 2016 shall comply with Tier III NOx standards. For engines with rated speed n < 130 RPM, maximum allowable NOx is 3.4 g/kWh. For 130 <= n < 2,000 RPM, allowable NOx is calculated by the formula: NOx = 9.0 * n^(-0.2) g/kWh. For n >= 2,000 RPM, maximum allowable NOx is 2.0 g/kWh. Exemptions apply during documented emergency sea-rescue operations or combat operations under Directive 13.5.4.

## SOLAS Chapter II-2 Regulation 4 - Fuel Oil Flashpoint Safety

SOLAS Chapter II-2, Regulation 4.2.1: No fuel oil with a flashpoint of less than 60°C (140°F) closed cup test shall be used, except for emergency generators where fuel with a flashpoint not less than 43°C may be permitted. Fuel storage tanks must be located outside Category A machinery spaces and separated from boilers by cofferdams of minimum 600 mm width.

## ClassNK Hull Integrity and Weld Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Section 8.2 Hull Butt Welds: 100% of full-penetration butt welds within the 0.4L midship region subject to peak bending moments must undergo phased array ultrasonic testing (PAUT) and radiographic testing. Allowable planar flaw length shall not exceed 4.0 mm, and crack-like indications of any depth are grounds for mandatory gouging and re-welding under Section 8.4 remediation protocol.
