## Auxiliary Desalination Reverse Osmosis Plant RO-4 (P.1)

Technical Manual Section 9.1.1 - Auxiliary Desalination Reverse Osmosis Plant RO-4 (Part 1):
Detailed operating procedure and maintenance instructions for shipboard installations. Freshwater production membrane fouling, chlorine injection ppm limits, high-pressure booster pump triplex ceramic plunger wear. Step 1: Inspect mechanical interfaces and confirm operational clearance within nominal tolerance of ±0.05 mm. Verify secondary containment integrity and ensure environmental drainage seals are certified to Level 4 marine resistance. Reference maintenance card MRC-100-001 for full technician signoff.
