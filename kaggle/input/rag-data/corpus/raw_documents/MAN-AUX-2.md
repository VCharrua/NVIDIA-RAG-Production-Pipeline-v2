## Sonar Dome Pressurization & Transducer Array Housing (P.1)

Technical Manual Section 9.2.1 - Sonar Dome Pressurization & Transducer Array Housing (Part 1):
Detailed operating procedure and maintenance instructions for shipboard installations. Rubber window acoustic degradation, internal pressurization nitrogen blanketing at 1.8 bar, synthetic boot elastomer bonding. Step 1: Inspect mechanical interfaces and confirm operational clearance within nominal tolerance of ±0.05 mm. Verify secondary containment integrity and ensure environmental drainage seals are certified to Level 4 marine resistance. Reference maintenance card MRC-101-001 for full technician signoff.
